   2) Public Website Test Cases
   ----------------------------
   For each page below, open the page and check the listed items. 
   If you cannot access a page, note the URL and the error.

   a) Home page (`/`)
   - Page loads and images appear.
   - Carousel slides advance and links work.
   - Programme cards show title and image.
   - Latest updates section shows recent items.

   b) About pages (`/about` and `/about/rti`)
   - Leadership messages and contact details are visible.
   - RTI page shows statutory information.

   c) Academics (`/academics`)
   - UG and PG sections listed.
   - Documents or syllabus links open or download.
   - Academic calendar PDF link works.

   d) Departments (`/departments`)
   - Department sections load and anchors jump correctly.

   e) Co-Curricular (`/co-curricular`)
   - Activities and NSS section visible.

   f) Facilities (`/facilities`)
   - Facility cards show icon, title, and description.
   - Contact CTA navigates to the Contact page.

   g) Placements (`/placements`)
   - Placed student cards show image and caption.

   h) Add-On Courses (`/add-on-courses`)
   - Course groups and lists visible.

   i) Admission (`/admission`)
   - Admission phone is shown and contact CTA works.

   j) Contact (`/contact`)
   - Contact details visible.
   - Enquiry form fields: name, email, type, message.
   - Submit shows a success message (if configured, email is sent).

   3) Search Testing
   -----------------
   - Open the search box and search for:
      - "admission", "BCA", "psychology", "placements", "contact"
   - Verify results are useful and links open correct pages.
   - Try an unknown word (e.g., "abcdxyz") — verify a friendly "no results" message appears.

   4) Downloads and Documents
   --------------------------
   - Click PDF links (syllabus, mandatory disclosures). The PDF should open or download.
   - If a PDF fails to open, note the URL and the error.

   5) Mobile Testing
   -----------------
   Use a mobile device or browser device emulator.
   - Open the menu and navigate to pages.
   - Check that text fits and buttons are big enough to tap.
   - Ensure images do not overlap text.

   6) Desktop Testing
   ------------------
   - Open pages on a larger screen.
   - Check layout, spacing, and that no elements overflow or break.

   7) Accessibility and Readability
   --------------------------------
   - Images should have meaningful alt text.
   - Try tabbing through the page with keyboard — you should be able to reach links and buttons.
   - Text must be readable (font size and color contrast).

   10) Bug Reporting Guide
   ----------------------
   When you find an issue, include clear steps so the team can reproduce it.

   Severity levels
   - Critical: site is down or core workflows (admissions/enquiry/admin login) broken.
   - High: important feature broken (file uploads fail, pages missing content).
   - Medium: visual issues or non-blocking functional problems.
   - Low: typos, small UI suggestions.

   Bug report template
   - Title: Short summary
   - Severity: Critical / High / Medium / Low
   - Steps to reproduce: 1, 2, 3
   - Expected result
   - Actual result
   - Page URL
   - Device / browser
   - Screenshot(s)
   - Time & date

   Example
   -------
   Title: "Contact form submit shows success but no email"
   Severity: High
   Steps:
   1. Open `/contact` on localhost
   2. Fill the form and submit
   Expected: Email arrives to college contact
   Actual: Success shown, no email received
   Page URL: http://localhost:3000/contact
   Device: Chrome on macOS
   Screenshot: attached
